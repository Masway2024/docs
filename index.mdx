## Welcome to AgriSense AI

AgriSense AI is a mobile application that empowers smallholder farmers across Africa with instant crop disease detection and treatment recommendations using artificial intelligence.

## The problem we solve

Smallholder farmers across Africa lose up to 40% of crops annually due to:

- Late detection of crop diseases
- Limited access to agricultural extension officers
- Lack of real-time advisory services
- Expensive and inaccessible diagnostic solutions

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Take a photo of a crop leaf using your mobile device
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze">
    Vertex AI Vision analyzes the image and detects diseases
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recommend">
    Google Genkit generates localized prevention and treatment steps
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act">
    Get instant recommendations and find nearby agro-vet shops
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Technology stack

AgriSense AI is built with cutting-edge technologies:

- **Vertex AI Vision** - Image classification and disease detection
- **Android Development Kit (ADK)** - Native mobile application
- **Google Genkit** - AI-generated recommendations and advisory
